Before leaving · applications, travel and follow-through
  • Reopen the role and confirm the right location. Keep the submitted version and confirmation.
  • Recheck opening hours and any special closure. Confirm a manager’s preferred time when possible.
  • Save outbound and return journeys for the selected date, starting and ending at Clarkson GO. Allow a missed-train buffer.
  • Bring a few tailored résumé copies, your availability, phone/charger, transit payment and any requested certification you actually hold.
  • Open with a 20-second introduction. Ask before taking more time; leave promptly if service is busy.
  • Log the person, outcome and any requested follow-up immediately in the restaurant record.
