# The Queen's charms — evidence

Captured on branch `claude/queen-charms` from `origin/main@7b26786` (#471, the Queen's world) with `node test/queen-charms-layout.mjs` (Chromium with `--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader` for the WebGL sections and `--disable-3d-apis --disable-gpu` for the no-WebGL section; `HEARTH_CHROMIUM` pointed at the container's Chromium). Every household is fictional: `planLifeFixture("household")` renamed "Fictional household", members "Alex (fictional)" and "Sam (fictional)"; the acts that earn charms (a fictional August Chapter held ten times and closed still-forming at a Sitdown, a fictional Charter signed by both, a fictional weekend-trip goal filled and bought) are seeded through the ordinary commands. No real books, no hosted rows.

`records.json` holds 35 records. Every width (320×568, 320×700, 390×844, 720×900, 1100×800) × the 3D path and the flat path × an empty bin, a few charms (390 and 1100) and the maximum of sixteen: 0 horizontal overflow, 0 vertical page scroll, the same count of charms on her in the still's words, in the drawn figure and (3D) as instances in the world, at most two draw calls per kind in use, 0–2 frames while nothing changes. The bench in 3D at 1100 and 390 and flat at 390: twelve in the bin, a cat pressed from it onto a free seat and selected, pressed by pointer onto her right flank, a press on her eye and on the belly's fill window leaving it where it sat, kept after a pause with the presser's name, the bench in keyboard order (Move → Turn → Smaller → Bigger → Lean → colours) with a ≥3px ring on each, ArrowUp walking the charm, 0 serious/critical axe hits. Forced colours (emulated) with easy read: the flat path, all sixteen charms drawn as shapes. A Chromium with no WebGL: `auto` yields silently at every width with sixteen charms drawn, and with none and a few at 390.

Frame timings are from SwiftShader (CPU rendering) in the cloud container, not a phone, and are an upper bound on CPU cost rather than a GPU figure: steady-state frames 1.6–5.1 ms with sixteen charms, first frames 549–663 ms with sixteen charms versus 399–549 ms with none (shader compilation plus building the kinds in use). In Node: all twelve kinds build in ~62 ms (26,580 non-indexed vertices across the library; a merged body and a merged ink accent per kind), sixteen charms lay out in ~16 ms the first time and ~0.4 ms thereafter, and the worst-case slide search (a press that will not take) costs ~0.4 ms.

The PNGs here are a curated subset; regenerate the full set with `HEARTH_ARTIFACTS_DIR=.artifacts/queen-charms node test/queen-charms-layout.mjs`.
