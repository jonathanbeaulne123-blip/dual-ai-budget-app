### Violations

| Rule | Impact | Selector | Themes | Fixed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color-contrast` | serious | `.path-recipe > .path-recipe__top > .path-recipe__fired` (×20, one per recipe) | Taylor | Yes. The count was `--theme-accent` (#946522) on the card (#f7efda): 4.41:1. | `target-size` | serious | `button[aria-label="Mar, Lean · uphill"]` (a month mark) | Taylor, Newfoundland (1100) | Yes. The mark sat partly under the top-right control cluster, so it showed but could not be fully pressed. `applyAnchors` now treats the controls, rail, Where we are / Next Move / tent buttons and the open card as space that is already taken. ### Not decided by axe (`incomplete`)

`color-contrast` came back "needs review" on 6 to 58 nodes per run. These are mark labels and chips over the canvas or over `color-mix` and gradient backgrounds, which axe cannot sample. Mark labels have their own opaque `--card` background (`--ink` on `--paper` for "now"). The sunrise chip is fixed at #5a3a12 on #fff1d6 (about 9:1). Nobody has measured these pixel by pixel.

### Visible effect of the target-size fix

Marks that land under a control now wait until the camera moves, just as overlapping labels already did. With a card open at 390px, the card covers most of the stage. There, 2 marks show instead of 7; at 1100, 11 show instead of 15. With no card open, the evidence set shows 8 marks at 320, 9 at 390, 17 at 720, 21 at 1100 and 23 at 1440. "Everything on the island" still lists every place.

## 2. Keyboard (Taylor, world live, 1100, Region)

From "Name our island together", Tab walks through:

1. The 21 visible marks in list order: months oldest to newest, then "We are here", the Chapter campfire, Our Charter, the two landmarks, the kiln, three clouds, the "?" signpost, the Plan Studio tent and Hercules's cottage.
2. Lantern (Dim/Warm/Bright), Quality (Full/Lite), layers (Weather/Story/Rhythm/Mine).
3. The distance rail (Sky/Region/Stop/Up close, Move closer, Move away).
4. Where we are, Next Move, "Open the Plan Studio tent".
5. Replay, the month slider, "Open this month in the time machine", then the three `<summary>` panels.

- Every visible mark was reached (21 of 21). No hidden mark took focus.
- Every stop showed a focus ring of at least 2px (checked with `getComputedStyle`; for a mark, the ring is on its label).
- **Focus rings (fixed):** before, only `.path-mark` had an explicit `:focus-visible` rule. Now there is one rule for every island control: `.path-world :is(button, summary, input, select, .path-world__card):not(.path-mark):focus-visible`, using `var(--world-focus, var(--theme-accent, var(--copper)))`. The mark rule uses the same token, and the button's own UA outline is turned off so the ring does not show twice.
- **Enter on a mark** opens its card. **Focus moves into the card (fixed).** Before, the card came last in DOM order, so Tab had to pass every remaining mark and all the controls to reach it. Now the card follows the marks in the DOM, and when a card is opened from the keyboard (`:focus-visible` on the opener) it takes focus (`tabIndex=-1`). Tab then goes to its Close button and actions. A mouse click leaves focus where it was.
- **Escape closes the card (added).** Focus goes back to the mark, outline row or Next Move button that opened it. If that element is gone and focus was inside the card, focus goes to "Where we are". Covered by a jsdom test and by the browser run (`focusAfterEscape: month:1`).
- **Tent:** Enter on "Open the Plan Studio tent" moves focus to "Back to the island". Enter there moves focus back to "Open the Plan Studio tent". (Unchanged; verified.)
- **Distance rail (fixed):** it used `aria-current="true|false"`, but it is a set of toggles like the lantern, so it now uses `aria-pressed`. The CSS selector was updated to match.
- **Next Move name (fixed):** the accessible name read "Next MoveChoose which payday…". A space now separates the two parts.

## 3. Every place is reachable

`test/our-path-world-ui.test.ts`, "Every place on the island is reachable from the DOM (a11y pass)". The test uses the fake world and a fictional household that has one of every kind. It checks that every id in the last `setScene` input has both an outline row (`data-place`) and a mark button. The kinds are campfires (within the grown months), moves, goals, lamps, memories, weather (clouds, storm, sunrises, mist), unknown, stones, footpaths, bridges, forks, charter, kiln, cottage and name. It also checks the reverse (every outline row has a mark) and that every mark has an `aria-label`.

No gaps were found. `data-place` was added to the marks and the outline rows so the test can match them; nothing else changed.

## 4. Known limits and open questions

- **Tent mark cut off at 390:** "Plan Studio · Hercules has a suggestion" can sit partly off the left edge of the stage (`overflow: hidden`). axe did not flag it. Still open.
- **No real screen reader:** VoiceOver, NVDA and TalkBack were not tested. Announcements (the card's `aria-live="polite"` together with the focus move) have not been heard.
- **One 404 per browser:** the first page of each browser logged "Failed to load resource: 404". Playwright's response events did not show it; it is most likely `/favicon.ico` on the proof server. It is recorded in the capture checks rather than filtered out.
- **No storm month in the hard story:** the fictional hard habitat has no month with the "Storm" character in its window. `taylor-hard-stop-1100.png` shows the steepest month ("Jul · Lean · uphill") instead, and INDEX.md says so.
