## Design direction

Lower road: warm stone, orchard shade, visible openings and a clear Foot arrival. Middle road: woodland, bridges and framed water views, with rail and lantern details outside the riding line. Upper road: more open meadow and wind-shaped planting, deliberate parapet runs and a calm Summit Commons arrival. Stops must be usable places beside the route, with a view and a route connection, never decoration in the lane.

Classic uses local stone, timber, brass lanterns and restrained planting; Taylor uses authored paper/card construction, stitched edges and garden-like groups; Newfoundland uses granite, salt-grey timber/metal, heath and wind-shaped trees. Lite removes secondary objects. All share the same physical road, financial destinations and shared 6/2 light pool.
