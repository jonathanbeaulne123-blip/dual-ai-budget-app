# Physical funicular and gondola controls

Jonathan asked for player interaction and movement on both rides, with controls presented as 3D buttons rather than tabs or word buttons. This work is isolated on `codex/ride-controls-3d` from `origin/main@0f601b55`; it does not merge, deploy, alter hosted data or touch financial commands.

The boarding arrows are meshes at the actual stations, with one arrow for each adjacent stop. Their click path verifies that the player is on the platform. The cabin carries the avatar along its existing spline while local walking input is clamped inside the cabin footprint; walking stands a seated gondola rider. A separate raised carriage arrow finishes the ride, and the gondola has a seat button. The world still uses the existing ride camera and arrival behavior. The travel guide no longer contains the funicular/gondola boarding form, and the floating text ride ticket and skip button are gone. Physical controls have Classic, Taylor and Newfoundland material treatments. F/Enter at a platform, F in the cabin and G for the gondola seat are keyboard equivalents; a screen reader status describes them.

Risk: **Medium-High** because raycasting, carriage attachment, camera and shared walking input change. Budget (5): **+0**; no money meaning or writer changes. Engagement (3): **+2 intended** through embodied boarding and movement.
