# The Highlands — how it looks (The Water's Way PR 3, module `crown`)

**SwiftShader, not device evidence.** Headless Chromium on the review harness (`horizon-review.html`, 2026-06-21),
960 × 600, taken by `scripts/horizon/capture-highlands.mjs` on the branch's final bake. `captures.json` lists every
shot with its pose, time and the frame's draw calls and triangles.

What was shot: Classic full — every pose at dawn (06:30, the place's story hour) and night (22:40), and Sketchbook pages
E and F at 10:30; Taylor's Scrapbook and Newfoundland full, and all three dressings lite — the porch and three signature
poses (Westwatch Chapel, Bench Hamlet, Fallswatch along the Veil) at dawn and night. 70 shots, 2.3 MB.

## In plain words

- **The porch** (`*_porch-north-pass_*`): you come over the north pass on the hamlet lane; Westwatch Chapel stands on
  its knoll with the sea behind, its verdigris bell cote on the north gable. The roadside dyke runs down the lane's
  west side. The chapel now stands on its door step's ground (it was sunk 1.3 m into the knoll's slope).
- **Westwatch Chapel** (`*_westwatch-chapel_*`): the long side, three lit slit windows at night, the cote above the
  ridge, the churchyard wall in front. Taylor gives it the scalloped eave and plum slates; Newfoundland white walls and a
  dark roof.
- **Bench Hamlet** (`*_bench-hamlet_*`, `*_bench-hamlet-to-horn_*`): nine crofts along the lane on the shelf, each with
  its walled yard, kale bed, hay and washing; strip walls running back; a fank; shelter birches. Four crofts are lit at
  night. The dry-stone walls no longer stand as tall slabs where they ran down the shelf's edge (they stop where the
  ground is steeper than 0.22).
- **Fallswatch** (`*_fallswatch-veil_*`, `*_fallswatch-deck_*`, `*_fallswatch-oak_*`): the deck on the west buttress
  with its open rails, the Veil viewer (free lever), the seated viewer, the panel, the bench and the island lantern;
  the view along the Veil's curtain. The oak telephoto shows the line toward the Green. The Old Oak itself is drawn by
  the Green's module, which is not on this branch.
- **High Shieling Ranch** (`*_high-shieling-ranch_*`): the longhouse and barn in the east valley under Mountain v2's
  dam wall, with the window's pool at night.
- **The drag lift and the Twin Tarns** (`*_drag-lift_*`, `*_twin-tarns_*`): the short tow's two towers, the foot
  station and its wheel pylon, the rope with T-bars, shore stones on the tarn's shingle.
- **The Rim Walk and the Rim Lookout** (`*_rim-walk-cairns_*`, `*_rim-lookout_*`): cairns along the walk toward the
  horn; the new lookout wall on the east lip with its low lamp, seen from above the Rim Steps.
- **Bight lookout → Westwatch** (`*_bight-to-westwatch_*`): the telephoto story link 7 → 1. The cote reads on the knoll.
- **Aerial** and **pages E / F**: the whole Highlands in context.

## Rough, honestly

- Much of what fills these frames is the land, not the dressing: the striped strata cliffs, the grey walk beds and
  their retaining walls, the land's railings by the chapel, the Rim Steps, v2's dam. Those are the land PR's.
- Three crofts on the shelf's edges stand on 1.2–1.8 m plinths. The kit draws the plinth dark, so from the lane it reads
  as a black band (`*_bench-hamlet-to-horn_*`). The flat ground holds eight crofts within 1.2 m; the ninth takes a
  steeper site.
- The Fallswatch frames along the Veil draw about 470 calls on full and 445 on lite. The same view drew about 460 before
  this pass (the Veil's cards and the lake). The ranch at night drew 517 once (103 at dawn, same pose). Both are over
  the 400 / 180 on-screen guide and need a device look.
- The moraine is sparse: most of the prototype's moraine ring lies on Mountain v2's own footprint, which this module
  never dresses.
- The lift's towers read near-black in Classic, and its stations are plain stone huts. That is the kit's look.
