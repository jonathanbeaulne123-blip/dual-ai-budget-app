# Mountain V3 — what to look at

Captures from the prototype harness (`/home/claude/proto`, three.js in headless Chromium with SwiftShader) over the **real baked terrain, water and beds** of this branch (`after-*.png`) and of `main` before the work (`before-*.png`). Fall curtains in these pictures are the harness's own sheets drawn from the same `FallCut` lip/top/foot numbers the runtime uses; buildings are not drawn (owed). Not device evidence. Fictional Development world; no household data.

| Pair | Camera | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| `aerial` | south-west, high | The Shoulder's bare ring is a highland: Glacier Peak and its snowfield behind v2's summit, the benches stepping down to the Hollow, Rillcut and Long Falls on the west wall, the Veil across the lake face, Stair Falls and the ranch on the backside, the Rim Bridge ridge at the right. |
| `west` | from the Hollow looking north-east | Before: one blank cliff. After: two 50 m curtains (Rillcut Falls at the corner, Long Falls in its box notch) with their pools at the wall foot; the hamlet's bench line above; the glacier's snout at the skyline. |
| `lake` | from Stillwater looking north | Before: the Shoulder's flat lake face. After: Veil Falls fills the bay between its buttresses, Fallswatch sits on the spur above; Spur Falls and Rillcut Falls read at the left; the glacier and the horn behind v2's crest. |
| `backside` | from the north-east over the Prow | Before: open heath between Horizon Drive and v2. After: the Rim Bridge ridge over the Drive with the Rim Steps ribbon, Split Wall Gorge carrying the beck to Stair Falls, High Shieling Ranch's bench and the Shieling Mere, the glacier walk and Col Steps up to the ice. |
| `plan` | straight down | The water network as one tree from the springs; the Year Walk's re-route past the pools; the new walks. |

Numbers behind the pictures: `docs/horizon/MOUNTAIN_V3.md` §4 (proofs) and the bake diagnostics (`public/horizon/world/horizon-geo-1.index.json.gz`, ids `cross.*` / `junction.cross.*` for the V3 footbridges and the Rim Steps over V01).
