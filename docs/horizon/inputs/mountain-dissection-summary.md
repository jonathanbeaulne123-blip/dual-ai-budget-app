# Mountain v2 dissection — what went wrong last time (source of NOT-THIS)
(from claude/hearth-mountain-ux-dissection-2026-09-24.md)
- Terrain: 9 cones + flat discs + a sine ripple + trenches; districts as flat discs on 0.8-slope cones; 19% steeper than 56°; summit sheared by an edge fade; no gorge (2–9 u); river floats up to 16.8 u; sea shows through inland.
- Roads: a "ladder" of 5 near-straight E–W traverses with hairpins; zero-thickness FrontSide ribbons; no kerb/parapet/edge collision; 544/890 u of road edge with a drop > body height; destinations 18–24 u off the road on 2-point planks aimed at building centres (40–81° off the door); no district-to-district path; no stairs; plots 30–45 u off road with no link (one at 73% grade).
- Structures: dam buried (5/16 u above the rim) in a pit with a land bridge through the basin; reservoir furniture inside the basin; skill branches as rail-less planks up to 26 u in the air, longer than the road they skip; invisible grind rails; floating pennants and tori; text planes without posts; labels hanging in the sky; gondola cable bowing upward.
- Movement: town→mountain blocked by a 1.4 u road lip; no downhill on the board (travel assist everywhere); finish gate in the sea; invisible dead-stop walls; rider not in the cabin; no slope term (77° cliffs climbable); edge teleports; feet off ground on the phone lattice; Retry cancelled the race; boarding as a form + teleport; funicular reversing direction 7×.
- Camera: no camera could look above the horizon (the dam and mountain never in frame); walking camera 29° down; handoffs whipping 190 u; leaving the Library parked the camera in town; no ride camera; zoom-out ejecting to Journey; overlook poses facing away from buildings; Look ignoring terrain (eye under ground).
- Art: grass-coloured cone trees, uniform scatter with bald rings around districts, bullseye rings of flowers, districts differing by nearest-centre tint + 0–7 props, box+pyramid storefronts, glassless Glasshouse, sun lighting slopes head-on, no shadows, fog too far, district pop-in at 75/99 u synchronous.
- The Skate v2 painted-card kit existed and the mountain used none of it.
Verdict: keep the contracts (WorldDefinition, surface/collision queries, 3D gates, versioned presence, streaming, BasinReading, race scaffolding, Skate v2 sim + card kit); re-author the place.
