## Recovery and product behavior

Local durable review records are saved before submission and keyed by account identity, environment, household, actor and creation context. Storage failure prevents submission. The creation input (including the reviewed clay baseline) and confirmation ID survive reload. The link has its own immutable reviewed operation and separate confirmation ID. A rejected link preserves the accepted bank, and the person may explicitly review a new link version. Unknown or pending acknowledgement retains its original identity.

A transport wait yields after five seconds; there is no polling loop. The person can return with the review saved, check again, or leave the room/native scene. A scope switch aborts receipt reads and prevents stale UI/command callbacks; each original scope retains its own recovery record. Unsubmitted creation may be cancelled back to the existing/new-bank choice. An uncertain submitted creation remains recoverable before selecting a different bank.

Only authenticated accepted receipts cause the created-bank callback or link completion. A fulfilled command promise alone does not imply success. Callback handoff persists the accepted goal before the creation record is cleared. On reload, the accepted creation is recovered independently from an unfinished link. A later missing/deleted bank is shown unavailable, never guessed from another goal. Browser history keeps the exact originating intention path and focus; successful linking returns there.

Creating a bank adds its canonical goal and initial clay; it posts no contribution, allocation, expense or transfer. Connecting the bank posts no IDs and leaves the financial audit hash unchanged. The existing contribution/earmark/review/Final Confirm paths remain separate.
