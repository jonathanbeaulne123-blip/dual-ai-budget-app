# Onboarding Slice 17 — Chapter 8 earning cadence contract

## Completion

Chapter 8 is member-required and self-owned. Each active member completes their
own probe only after recording one valid earning cadence:

- weekly;
- every other week;
- twice a month;
- monthly; or
- no fixed rhythm.

The Household chapter is ready only when every active member has their own
valid cadence. One person's job or schedule never completes the other person's
probe.

## Shared timing and Personal detail

The cadence is a small Shared household planning fact. Job identity, rate,
deductions, landing account, shift detail, and income amount remain Personal
and are deferred to the later work-details chapter. Choosing “I'll add those
details later” records explicit progress without creating a job, income row,
account, transaction, journal entry, or assumed contribution.

## Irregular cadence

“No fixed rhythm” is first-class valid data, not a blank or error state. It
survives shaping and Shared continuity, satisfies the member probe, and must not
produce an invented next payday, calendar tick, amount, or Fund contribution.

## Evidence and authority

- Evidence is produced only from the viewing member's cadence record.
- The visible sentence names the member and timing, but no rate, amount,
  employer, account, shift detail, or partner-Personal fact.
- Only the member may record or change their own cadence.
- Acknowledgement re-projects current evidence before writing only that
  member's Personal onboarding progress.
- The current cloud-ledger authority boundary remains unchanged.

## UX

Hercules routes to the existing Shift surface. The member answers one soft,
five-choice question, sees a plain-language preview, and explicitly saves.
Date/day details appear only where the selected cadence needs them. The form
contains no money amount or employment-detail field.

## Kill criteria

- one member's cadence completes the other member;
- an irregular cadence is treated as missing or creates a projected date/tick;
- Hearth assumes an amount, contribution, job, rate, deduction, account, or
  shift detail;
- partner detail appears in evidence;
- skipping details creates an income or money record;
- rendering, routing, or selecting a choice acknowledges the chapter;
- the cadence path bypasses Shared acceptance or writes another member's fact.
