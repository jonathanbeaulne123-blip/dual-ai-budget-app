## Prompt set

Generation direction shared by the scenes: landscape 1536×1024 original editorial illustration for a themed ledger website; richly detailed semi-realistic gouache and fine ink, subtle brushwork, coherent lighting, believable scale and material detail. Full bleed, no UI mockup, logos, watermark or legible text. One coherent setting with rich upper/right detail and a quieter lower-left reading surface. Avoid flat clip art, floating icon collections and artificial CGI.

**Classic:** Sunlit household bookkeeping at an oak kitchen desk and window. Open clothbound ledger with fine ivory ruling but no readable writing, brass clips, pencil, ceramic coffee, linen coaster and faint coffee ring. Trailing pothos in cream ceramic and a terracotta herb. Kitchen shelf and cream wall. Show wood grain, stitching, page edges, glaze, linen weave, leaf veins and soft leaf shadows. Sage, cream, warm oak, terracotta and restrained brass.

**reputation:** Layered black-and-white newspaper and black leather/vinyl, with a detailed gold serpent left and red serpent right. The initial generation drifted into antique sepia and was discarded. A built-in image edit retained the composition and snakes while replacing cathedral etchings, wax seals, astrology, magnifier and florals with sharp contemporary photocopied newsprint, oversized abstract blackletter fragments, modern urban halftone, silver chain and black satin. Plain black leather notebook instead of a gold medieval grimoire. Black, cool charcoal and off-white; gold and crimson are the strong colour accents. No readable lyrics, headlines, people, logos or tickets.

**TTPD:** Melancholy writing room at a worn warm-grey desk beside a misty multipane window. Ivory typewriter, indistinct manuscripts, letters, dark wax seal, pressed flowers, porcelain coffee, cream candles in tarnished brass, cloth book, moth and notebook. Ivory linen to the left. Graphite, taupe and parchment. Typewriter keys, paper fibres, ceramic detail, wax, stems and weave. Bright enough for reading; no horror treatment or people.

**Harbour:** Working St. John’s on a clear cool afternoon. Close timber wharf looking through the Narrows, rocky hills, clustered wooden houses and a distant tower silhouette. Blue-green ripples and reflections; weathered red fishing boat with cream wheelhouse, mooring rope, rusty bollards, pier ladders and small cargo details. Pale blue, sea glass, ochre, brick and chalk white. Coastal working harbour rather than tropical marina; interpreted geography rather than a surveyed view.

**Merchant:** A cherished dinner interpreted as an intimate cream-plaster and dark-walnut interior. Long polished table, brass sconces and pendants, framed coastal paintings, blue ribbed glasses, ivory linen and silver cutlery. Three golden seared scallops with herbs in the near-right foreground and warm candlelight. Room detail above and to the right, calm foreground wood to the left. Wood grain, glass reflections, scallop searing, linen texture and shadows. Walnut, honey, ivory, sea glass and olive. Not an exact restaurant floorplan.

## Presentation limits

Desktop keeps a still scene beside the scrolling reading sheet. Full-page screenshots cannot represent a sticky background at every scroll position; separate scrolled viewport captures document the continuing scene. Merchant includes a CSS-framed close view of the same scallop illustration in its title. Phone uses the whole landscape image, with title and controls outside the essential illustrated detail. Motion is separately layered, localized and subject to persistent pause, reduced motion, focus quieting and visibility suspension.
