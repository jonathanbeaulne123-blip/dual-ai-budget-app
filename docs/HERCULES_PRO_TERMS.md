Last updated: 2026-08-25

Hercules Pro is an optional Development companion for teaching and explaining information already posted in Hearth. It is not a bank, accountant, tax preparer, financial adviser, payment service, or emergency service. Its answers may be incomplete or wrong and should be checked against the linked Hearth source facts before making a financial decision.

The connection is read-only. Hercules Pro cannot add, edit, delete, post, pay, transfer, merge, sync, or move money. A human must return to Hearth and use its ordinary Confirm flow for any change.
