## Common acceptance contract

Every app change starts as a private draft, receives an editable review bound to scope and accepted source facts, and requires its Final Confirm button or the exact standalone command. The authority revalidates and consumes the private claim with the domain result. Duplicate/retried requests use their original identity. Resuming a draft never carries review authority. Unresolved submissions retain their identity; cancellation races with submission on the same claim.

For every command adapter, acceptance means: screen/chat parity; missing inputs and ambiguous targets remain unresolved; changed source facts invalidate review; no domain mutation before confirmation; exact receipt recovery; permitted actor/view only. These are required tests, not an assertion that each operation has passed.
