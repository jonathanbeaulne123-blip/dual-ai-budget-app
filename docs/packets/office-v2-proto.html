Hearth · Office v2 · working prototype

A desk, not a catalog

Home today is seventeen equal rows and 1,391px of scroll. This is the same app with five things on the desk, the number louder than the label, and everything else one tap away in a drawer. Nothing was deleted.

Three stamps carry the navigation: Post · Due · Close. Pending presses hard in copper and carries a real figure. Settled ghosts out to an em-dash. The desk underneath is then free to be short, because it has stopped doing the wayfinding.

Try it

  1. Tap Due — the bill instrument comes to the desk and opens.
  2. Tap any instrument to expand it in place. One at a time.
  3. Edit desk — jiggle mode. Cycle a size chip S→M→L, or × to send it to the drawer. The calculator has no ×; it can never leave.
  4. Drawer — every instrument Hearth has, live values, one tap to bring back.
  5. Look — four paper stocks, density, cat size. One overlay, never a second theme.
  6. Desks — Tracker · Kitchen · CPA · Play. A starting point, not a cage.
  7. More — where the parked features live. All still working.

Fixture data is the demo kitchen, read from the live build. Real-size phone frame: 390×844.
