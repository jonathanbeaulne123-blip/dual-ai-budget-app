## Sheets era (through `v0.0.31`)

Google Sheets + Apps Script. Git tag `sheets-v0.0.31`. Full snapshot: [../reference/sheets-era/](../reference/sheets-era/).

**Phases 0–5** (calendar dates in August–October 2026): stand up Git and clasp on development; full system review; stabilize Transaction Input, Add Shift, and the dashboard; planning/goals/recurring; Bianca beta; October freeze. The money laws that survived — CAD, Toronto dates, splits, transfers not spend, tip math that previews as posted, development ≠ production — are now the TypeScript command kernel. The spreadsheet runtime is gone.

We kept the honesty. We did not keep the 15-item menu.

## Cursor Chapters 0–5

The post-Sheets pitch: turn a kitchen ledger into a household family office.

| Chapter | It wanted | What actually happened |
|---|---|---|
| **0** Kitchen-table trust | Auth + RLS, boring correctness, sit-down, daily kitchen, Audit Office, Accounts Floor | Sit-down, kitchen, Audit Office, and Accounts Floor **shipped**. Auth + RLS did **not**. The chapter mixed “done” with “the door is still open.” |
| **1** Frictionless truth | CSV inbox, Open Banking, shift forecast, IOU → Interac | Still gated. Trailing-average shift pulse shipped as a look, not money. |
| **2** Autonomous liquidity | Safe-to-spend, tax lockbox, sweeps, goal acceleration | Vision only. |
| **3** Virtual family office | Issued cards, FX lines, family-office surface | Vision only. Blocked on Auth + a BIN sponsor. |
| **4** The protocol | BaaS / white-label after other households | Vision only. |
| **5** Continuous office | Expand every doc forever | Became overlapping bibles. Dual Course replaced the habit of adding another chapter. |

The destinations were mostly right. The **index** was not. A rival memo’s 18-month clock leaked into the living docs. Dual Course keeps the destinations and throws away the chapter numbers.

A second map for habit, stacked on the chapters.

| Ring | It wanted | What actually happened |
|---|---|---|
| **0** Daily kitchen | Chalkboard, Hercules, visit spark, Ask, wallet tiles | Shipped as The Hercules Update + Accounts Floor. |

| **3** Daily gravity | Lights-on, watch, widgets | Ideas, not a plan. Widgets still wait on true safe-to-spend. |

Rings were a good *description of shipped ritual*. They were a bad *spine for the company*. Course B now carries ritual; Course A still wins.

## Launch essays

Three product waves each grew a long essay that also tried to be the roadmap:

- **Hercules** — Neko body, Animal Crossing mouth, Finch care, Cleo tone, Clippy as anti-pattern. Ember retired. He never posts.
- **Audit Office** — show the GL to a room that met the cat. Opinion, statements, rec, close. Never a second ledger.
- **Accounts Floor** — bank-book tiles, not Mint envelopes or YNAB’s pretend Visa cash. Paydown remains a transfer.

Those products remain. The essays are here so we remember *why* they look this way. How to try them is in the short living files, not in this folder.

## What we refuse to forget (without treating it as “next”)

- Mint died because a feed plus guilt is not a habit.
- YNAB’s “give every dollar a job” is still a good lesson — on a journal, not instead of one.
- A pet that dies, or a pet you pay to keep alive, will never sit on this kitchen counter.
- Open Banking, Interac, issued cards, and BaaS were never stupid. They were early.
- The next honest dollar of engineering was always the hosted door, not Thursday’s tip model.
