## What made it “big thinking”

The map connected the project into one systems-level field:

1. Contain trust leaks and reconcile the delivery surface.
2. Complete the Bianca-ready local monthly loop.
3. Make two phones boring and lossless.
4. Auth + RLS and explicit cloud consent.
5. Reconciliation and safe intake.
6. Family-office-grade controls.
7. Learning, Hercules, and ambient household rhythm.
8. Settlement and external money coordination.
9. Cards, allowances, and regulated rails.
10. Other households and platform shape.

The artifact also carried current-truth gates, shipped capability chapters, a rival/inspiration matrix, AI tooling roles, Dual Course scoring, a recent worksession, and a source register.

## The durable insight

Hearth was no longer “a budget spreadsheet that might become an app.” It was becoming one household system with explicit ordering:

- protect the command and accounting boundary;
- finish the first truthful monthly loop;
- make multiple devices converge without loss;
- bind cloud access to identity and membership;
- add intake, controls, education, and external rails only through proven gates;
- let Hercules create rhythm without money authority.
