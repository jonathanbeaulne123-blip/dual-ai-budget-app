## What the map believed

The spreadsheet was the proving ground and a standalone app was still the final phase. Its nine displayed stages were:

1. Phase 0 — Application-Ready Data Architecture.
2. Phase 1 — Fix the Foundation.
3. Phase 1.5 — Everyday-Use Tooling & Code Health.
4. Phase 1.6 — Formula & Performance Hardening.
5. Phase 2 — Financial Engine.
6. Phase 3 — Dashboard 2.0.
7. Phase 4 — Goals & Fun.
8. Phase 5 — Automation.
9. Phase 6 — The App.

It recorded normalized transactions and stable IDs, raw-import staging, categories, accounts, members, monthly Budget Plan rows, duplicate adjudication, recurring/categorization schemas, quick-entry dialogs, monthly sit-down tooling, category-spending views, diagnostics, a change log, and the first Add Shift / Tip Tracker.

## What survived into Hearth

- The normalized-ledger instinct became typed commands and double-entry PGlite books.
- Monthly planning became the guided sit-down and budget commands.
- Add Shift became a first-class Shift surface with review-before-post boundaries.
- Formula diagnostics became Health, audit evidence, and fail-closed validation.
- Goals, dashboard context, import review, duplicate detection, and an assistant all survived in reshaped form.

## What did not survive

- Sheets formulas and Apps Script are not Hearth's runtime.
- Clasp is not a release path for Hearth.
- Historical completion counts do not measure current progress.
- “The App” is not a future Phase 6: the Hearth rebuild began on 2026-08-20.
