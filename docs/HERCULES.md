# Hercules

**The product face:** a living Maine Coon who wanders the kitchen, teaches in one breath, and never writes a cent.

The ledger is still the company (Dual Course weight 5). He is Course B (weight 3). Care is posted household facts, not a hunger meter. Ember was the flame prototype and is fully historical.

Laws: [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) D-042 / D-044 / D-045 / D-046 / D-047. Strategy: [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md). Museum essay: [nostalgia/HERCULES.md](nostalgia/HERCULES.md).

## Laws (do not change)

- No dock card. No full-screen Clippy sheet. He loafs in the corner while Add is open.
- Tap / chips = `talkHercules` / `askHercules` (journal-true). Typed chat = Workers AI on `POST /hercules/chat`. The model cannot post, emit SQL, invent a write, or name who spent more.
- Mood from Health, bills, goals, week-over-week spend. Punishment is a face and a sentence, never a fake fee.
- Unlocks come from posted facts, a tied rec, or a closed month — never a shop, never pay-to-keep-alive, never pet death.
- **Kill criterion:** if Bianca will not add a grocery because he is in the way, shrink him. Drag him. Pin him.

## Dual Course

Every new line in his mouth must point at a budget primitive (wallet, rec, sit-down, Health, D-016). Every new accounting surface should give him one honest sentence. If they conflict, the books win.

## How to try it

1. **App/website:** Hearth (kitchen site or `pnpm dev`)
2. **Tab/page:** any page — he wanders. Home for the net and opinion. Add to see him loaf.
3. **Instructions:**
   1. Open the demo kitchen table (Development).
   2. Watch him wander. Drag him off a number if he sits on it.
   3. Tap him. Ask **Opinion?** or **Working capital?** or **What’s on the Visa?** or “we good?” Type in the bubble. Hit **send**.
   4. Tap **+** — he loafs. Confirm still posts.
   5. If a bill is due he may pace. Calendar, then confirm.
   6. Tie a bank rec on Books to unlock **audit spectacles**. Close a month for the **green-ink stamp**. On Books he stretches for fieldwork.

Smug-kind Maine Coon on a Toronto counter: first person, short sentences, occasional mrrp, milk → bills → treats. He is also the household auditor. Dollar facts come from `talkHercules`, not from the model’s imagination. Chat history is this session only, not the household snapshot.
