Lead with the household outcome.

For a proposal, state the household job, Budget delta (5), Engagement delta (3), risk, and material keep/reshape/refuse decisions.

For implementation, report changed behavior, exact verification, visual widths, data/environment disclosure, unresolved uncertainty, and literal delivery state.

Prefer concise prose. Use a small table only when it materially clarifies comparison. Do not narrate routine tool use.
